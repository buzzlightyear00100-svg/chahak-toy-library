About Chahak

A toy one child has outgrown can be another child's favourite

Chahak (चहक) means the happy chirping of birds. We collect toys that families no longer use, clean and sort them, and open them up to children who would not otherwise have them. Every child who walks in gets to play, learn and imagine.

Free for children in need

Access to every toy and game is free for children from underprivileged families.

Learning through play

Science kits, puzzles and board games build curiosity, patience and problem-solving.

A second life for toys

Instead of gathering dust at home, toys keep moving from one child to the next.

How it works

From a donor's cupboard to a child's hands

  1. Families donate

    People across Sri Ganganagar bring in toys their children no longer use.

  2. We clean and sort

    Every toy is checked, cleaned and placed on the shelves by type.

  3. Children come to play

    Kids visit the library in Jawahar Nagar and play with whatever they love.

  4. The joy keeps moving

    Toys are shared from child to child, so nothing sits unused.

Questions

Parents often ask

Does it cost anything?

No. Access to all toys and games is free for children from underprivileged families.

Which ages is Chahak for?

All ages. We have baby walkers and stacking toys, ride-ons and ball pits, and board games and science kits for older children.

When can we visit?

Tuesday to Sunday, 10 am to 5 pm. The library is closed on Mondays.

How do I get updates about my child?

Message the caretaker on duty on WhatsApp at +91 70733 77843.

Can we celebrate a birthday at Chahak?

Yes. Bring a treat to share with the children and a toy to donate. Message Ms. Komal Aggarwal or Dr. Richa Periwal on WhatsApp to plan the day.

What toys can I donate?

Clean toys in good working condition, and board games and puzzles with all their pieces. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange a drop-off.
